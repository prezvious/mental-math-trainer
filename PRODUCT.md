# Mental Math Trainer

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Browser based arithmetic practice with configurable rounds, automatic answer checking, round summaries, and progress history. Product capabilities below are established by README.md and the existing implementation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Standard and mixed trainers support keyboard input and existing arithmetic operations and difficulty settings.
- Guest preferences and progress stay on the device; configured Supabase accounts can sync preferences and history.
- Interface designs, 59 color themes, and light/dark/system display modes are independently selectable.
- Eight additional selectable interfaces cover calculator, print, orbital, crossword, athletics, Bauhaus, field-journal, and handheld-game visual worlds with palette-adaptive SVG artwork.
- Studio remains the default interface. The user confirmed that Kira Kira is an additional selectable interface.
- Preserve round behavior, timers, shortcuts, navigation, account flows, and progress storage during design work.

## Brand Commitments

Mental Math remains the product name. The Kira Kira interface is inspired by the supplied Harajuku Kira Kira Burger packaging: outlined dimensional lettering, striped details, cheerful characters, and an orderly repeating pattern. The user allows any color combination and explicitly requires original characters adapted to mental math, with varied decorative objects rather than recreating the reference food characters or using only diamonds.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, current trainer/account/progress routes, and six user supplied packaging reference images with descriptive packaging notes. No new commercial claims or demonstration statistics are required.
