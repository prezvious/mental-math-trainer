# Körner introduction artwork

Scope: the decorative illustration in `components/PageIntro.js`, Operate mode.
The user wants much more relaxed, creative, high-quality SVG art, without a
studying theme. Keep the existing interface, copy, preferences, and round flow.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Slow Garden, an original sleeping cat nestled in an oversized ceramic
cup among sculptural foliage. The illustration supplies a quiet moment rather
than literal arithmetic or a student at a desk.

**OWN-WORLD:** Flowing organic contours, balanced ink outlines, ceramic bands,
botanical veins, and a warm low sun. Palette roles inherit the active theme;
the transparent SVG contains no raster image, external resource, or lettering.

**STORY:** Visitors meet a small, imaginative garden scene, then use the existing
trainer controls. Decorative artwork never competes with an active round.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A wide, self-contained illustration occupies the existing
right-hand intro column. The curled cat and cup form the dominant silhouette;
foliage frames it asymmetrically. Mobile retains a compact right-hand vignette.

**FORM:** A local illustration replacement within the established Körner system;
no concept seed or new visual identity is required. Static, relaxed artwork is
the signature; there is no perpetual motion.

finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
