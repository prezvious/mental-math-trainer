Theme supplied ink and input surfaces remain legible; softened candy regions adapt to light and dark paper.

## Motif exploration

Considered: number tiles, arithmetic operators, tally bundles, rulers, starbursts, abacus beads, graph fragments, compass arcs, paper clips, notebooks, progress steps, and pencil shavings. Selected flashcards/pencil/stopwatch as the character family for practice/thinking/speed. The five smaller motif families above provide alternating widths and varied spacing without mimicking the reference diamonds or introducing another decorative object for every gap.
